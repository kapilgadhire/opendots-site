FAQ

Questions teams ask about the platform

What is a customer experience platform?

A customer experience platform, or CX platform, is software that brings every place customers talk to a brand into one system, so the brand can answer, resolve and learn from all of it. Most customer experience software stops at collecting feedback or routing tickets. OpenDots is a customer experience management platform that does the answering as well: AI agents reply in your voice, resolve requests through your own systems, and turn every conversation into themes your team can act on.

What is OpenDots?

OpenDots is an AI-powered customer experience platform. It listens to every review, chat, social post and email your customers send, replies the way your best person would, and turns what it hears into themes your team can act on.

Which channels does OpenDots connect to?

Google reviews, App Store and Google Play reviews, delivery-app reviews, WhatsApp and live chat, Instagram, X and LinkedIn mentions, and your support email inbox. Each channel takes about five minutes to authorize.

How long does setup take?

One short guided call. We walk you through it, and your part is approving access for each channel, about five minutes apiece. OpenDots then reads your past conversations overnight, writes a playbook from your history, and is answering customers within 24 hours.

Will the replies sound like our brand?

Yes. OpenDots learns your voice from how your team has already replied: tone, phrasing, what you offer and when. Every reply is composed fresh and comes with a reasoning trace showing the scenario, rule and live facts behind it.

Do we stay in control of what gets sent?

Always. For each scenario you choose whether OpenDots sends on its own, drafts for approval, or hands the conversation to a person. Every edit your team makes folds back into the playbook.

What insights does OpenDots give the business?

Every closed conversation becomes a signal. OpenDots groups them into themes by channel and trend: problem areas that keep coming back, what customers love, and requests that are starting to grow, so each team knows where to look.
