FAQ

Frequently asked questions about voice of customer analytics

What is voice of customer analytics?

Voice of customer analytics turns what customers say about a brand, in reviews, social posts and support conversations, into structured insight that can be measured. It shows which issues customers raise, how often, and whether they are growing. A customer insights platform does this at scale. OpenDots Voice of Customer Analytics does it across reviews, social and support, in more than 30 languages, and counts every message rather than a sample.

What is a customer feedback platform?

A customer feedback platform brings feedback from every channel into one place, so a business can respond to it, analyze it and act on it. Some platforms collect survey responses. OpenDots works with the feedback customers already give: app store and Google reviews, social comments and DMs, and support conversations. It replies to that feedback and turns it into themes you can track.

How is this different from customer sentiment analysis?

Sentiment analysis labels a message positive, negative, or neutral. It tells you how customers feel, but leaves open what they are talking about. Conversely, Voice of Customer (VoC) Analytics sorts messages by meaning. OpenDots groups every message into a specific actionable theme, such as a delayed refund or a confusing checkout step, and counts it, so you know exactly what to fix and whether it is getting better.

How does OpenDots create themes?

OpenDots reads your feedback, infers what your business does, and builds a structure of categories, themes and specific feedback points from it. It groups messages by meaning, so ten thousand phrasings of one problem, in any language, land on one feedback point. As new patterns appear, the structure rebalances to hold them, and the themes you track stay comparable over time.

Does it work across languages?

Yes. OpenDots analyzes feedback in more than 30 languages natively and maps every language to the same themes, so a complaint written in Hindi and the same complaint written in English carry one count.

Can we track a theme over time?

Yes. Once a category or theme is discovered, it stays available for tracking. Make any of them a KPI, measured by volume and by share of all conversations, and compare it week over week or month over month, by channel and by team.

Do we need an integration to start?

Public reviews on Google Play, the Apple App Store and Google Business Profile can be analyzed with no integration, which is also how the free review audit works. Social accounts and support channels are connected when you want them in the same view.

Can we see the messages behind a number?

Yes. Every insight traces back to the original messages. Open any theme, or any point on a chart, and read the reviews, comments and conversations it counts.

How does analytics connect to replies and resolution?

OpenDots reads each message once. Response Intelligence replies to it, Voice of Customer Analytics counts it in a theme, and Agentic Resolution uses the same themes to resolve recurring requests through your own systems. Because all three share one structure, OpenDots can also tell when a public complaint began as a private conversation that was never closed.
