How it works

Five steps from your first connected channel to every customer answered, resolved and counted.

  1. 1

    Connect your channels

    Link your app stores, Google Business Profile, social accounts and support inbox. Public reviews need no integration at all.

  2. 2

    Set your voice and rules

    Choose a tone, add reference replies for your most common issues, and decide what always waits for a person.

  3. 3

    OpenDots learns your business

    It reads your past conversations, builds the themes that fit your business and drafts playbooks for recurring requests.

  4. 4

    Review and approve

    For the first one to two weeks your team checks replies and approves each playbook. Every edit tunes the next one.

  5. 5

    Go live and keep improving

    Replies post in your brand voice, routine requests resolve through your own systems, and themes become KPIs you track every week.

FAQs

How does OpenDots differ from traditional AI helpdesk bots?

OpenDots works as an AI agent for customer service, and does not stop at deflecting tickets. It reads public reviews and social feedback alongside support conversations, replies in your brand voice, resolves issues by calling your own internal systems, and counts every conversation so teams across your entire business see exactly what to fix.

Is OpenDots a social listening tool?

Yes, and an end-to-end resolution platform that actively resolves what it hears. OpenDots tracks signals across Instagram, X, app stores, and Google Business, then handles the critical actions listening tools miss: it replies in your brand voice, resolves issues directly through your internal systems, and turns every conversation into Voice of Customer analytics you can track quarter to quarter.

Which customer channels and platforms does OpenDots integrate with?

OpenDots integrates with public review platforms (Google Play, Apple App Store, Google Business Profile); social channels (Instagram comments and DMs, X mentions and hashtags, Facebook, Reddit); and private channels (WhatsApp, email, live chat, in-app support). Voice is not supported.

Does OpenDots replace our existing CRM and ticketing software?

No. OpenDots connects to your CRM and ticketing software through APIs rather than replacing them. It retrieves orders, refunds, account records, and business policies to resolve a conversation, then writes back through those same integrations. Your system of record stays where it is and remains yours.

How does the AI ensure responses match our specific brand voice?

OpenDots learns your brand voice directly from your communication history rather than a generic template. You define tone, formatting, emoji, and tagline rules, then review responses manually for the first one to two weeks. The platform analyzes every edit your team makes, tunes itself against those revisions, and only then transitions to full automation.

How do we control when the AI acts autonomously versus routing to a human agent?

You set the control level per playbook across three tiers. Draft composes the full reply and publishes nothing. Partial publishes informational replies, but holds system actions (like refunds or cancellations) for approval. Auto-response composes and resolves end to end. Sensitive interactions, including legal threats, VIP accounts, abuse, and 1- or 2-star reviews, never post without human sign-off.

Can the platform automatically reply to public app store reviews and local listings?

Yes. OpenDots automatically replies to Google Play, Apple App Store, and Google Business Profile reviews in your brand voice, applying specific rules based on the star rating. It also weaves your target App Store Optimization keywords into those replies, lifting store rankings and local search visibility over time rather than just closing the loop.

How does the Voice of Customer analytics categorize unstructured feedback?

OpenDots reads your feedback history and generates its own categories, themes, and feedback points automatically, so nobody maintains a taxonomy by hand. This goes further than customer sentiment analysis, which tells you the mood but not the cause. Ten thousand phrasings of one complaint collapse into a single countable issue, and that issue keeps the exact same definition next quarter so your metrics remain directly comparable.
